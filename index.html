Lab and work

Undergraduate researcher, SuperCDMS group

School of Physics and Astronomy · May 2026–present · UROP recipient

I'm one of the main operators of the group's Bluefors LD250 dilution refrigerator. I designed and built a heater mount for the 4 K stage, used it to load-test the stage at 0.54 W (3.49 K to 4.33 K), and then rewired the leads in phosphor bronze to cut the conducted heat load. I compiled a 13-part drawing package for another university's detector holder, got it quoted by our machine shop and turned their DFM feedback into design changes. I've also designed a sheet-metal bracket, a feedthrough plate the shop CNC-machined, and cryostat spacers that took seven print iterations. My current project, funded by a $1,800 UROP award, is redesigning a two-axis radioactive-source mover, and I'm helping revise the paper about it.

I'll add photos and drawings once the collaboration clears them.
