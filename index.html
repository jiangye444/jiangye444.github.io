Cryogenic detector hardware · SuperCDMS, University of Minnesota

Undergraduate researcher (UROP recipient) in Prof. Yan Liu's dark-matter group and one of the main operators of a Bluefors LD250 dilution refrigerator. I design and build what goes inside it: a 4 K-stage heater mount that I load-tested at 0.54 W (3.49 K → 4.33 K), heater leads rewired in phosphor bronze to cut conducted heat load, a 13-part drawing package I took through DFM with the machine shop, a sheet-metal bracket, a CNC-machined feedthrough plate, and seven printed iterations of cryostat spacers. Current project: redesigning a two-axis radioactive-source mover (funded by a $1,800 UROP award) and helping revise the paper that describes it. Photos and drawings will follow once the collaboration has cleared them.

Building controls and energy analysis · UMN Facilities, Energy Management

Student assistant on a 10+ building portfolio: Johnson Controls Metasys operator graphics, BACnet device work, a controls-upgrade drawing set for Peters Hall, chilled-water ΔT/flow trend analysis that flags low-ΔT coils, and a gas-use estimate for an art-building kiln room. Write-up to follow.
