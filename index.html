SuperCDMS · School of Physics and Astronomy

Cryogenic detector hardware

Undergraduate researcher (UROP recipient) in Prof. Yan Liu's dark-matter group and one of the main operators of a Bluefors LD250 dilution refrigerator. I designed and load-tested a 4 K-stage heater mount (0.54 W, 3.49 K → 4.33 K), rewired its leads in phosphor bronze to cut conducted heat load, took a 13-part drawing package through DFM with the machine shop, and built a sheet-metal bracket, a CNC-machined feedthrough plate and seven printed iterations of cryostat spacers. Now redesigning a two-axis radioactive-source mover ($1,800 UROP award) and helping revise the paper that describes it.

  • Cryogenics
  • Heat-load testing
  • Helium leak checks
  • DFM
  • Sheet metal
  • Design for CNC
